Research-Oriented University System

This console-based Java project models university users, academic workflows, research activity, support entities, reports and serialization.

Main Classes

ClassPurposeKey behavior
UserBase account type.Authentication fields, activation, password change, clone, serialization.
EmployeeBase employee type.Salary, employee id, messages and complaints.
AdminSystem administration.Add/update/deactivate users, add courses, view logs and reports.
ManagerAcademic office workflows.Approve registrations, assign teachers, manage news, generate reports.
TeacherCourse instruction.View courses, grade enrolled students, view student info.
StudentBachelor student model.Course registration, drop course, marks, transcript, teacher rating, research supervisor.
CourseCourse catalog item.Credits, major/year targeting, multiple instructors, lessons, seat reservation.
EnrollmentStudent course registration.Pending/approved/rejected/dropped states and mark link.
MarkAssessment result.First attestation, second attestation, final exam, total and letter grade.
LessonCourse lesson.Lesson type, topic, room and date/time.
ResearcherResearch role wrapper around a user.Papers, projects, h-index, supervisor validation, top cited calculations.
ResearchPaperResearch publication.Title, journal, DOI, date, pages, citations, authors.
ResearchProjectResearch project.Topic, participants, papers and non-researcher validation.
UniversitySystemApplication facade and singleton.Central workflow methods for users, courses, enrollments, research and reports.
DataStorageSerialization storage utility.Save and load serializable data.

Design Patterns

  • Factory: UserFactory creates user subclasses.
  • Singleton: UniversitySystem.getInstance() provides one central system instance.
  • Facade: UniversitySystem exposes high-level workflows for demo and roles.
  • Strategy: research comparators and ReportStrategy implementations switch sorting/report behavior.
  • Repository/Storage utility: DataStorage encapsulates serialization.

Important Rules

  • Students cannot register for more than 21 credits.
  • Students cannot register after three failed courses.
  • A 4th year student must have a research supervisor.
  • A research supervisor must have h-index at least 3.
  • Only researchers can join research projects.
  • Professor teachers are automatically registered as researchers in UniversitySystem.

Demo

Run university.demo.DemoApp. It demonstrates authentication, course registration, manager approval, grading, transcript, research papers, research projects, exceptions, reports and serialization.
